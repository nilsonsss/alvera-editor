## The style (Arthur Brooks "premium minimal"), 10 principles
1. Captions: sans (Inter) for normal words + serif italic (Playfair) for key words; per line (≤5 words) the key words go big/bold in the centre, the rest smaller in the gaps above/below.
2. B-roll matched to each script segment, with paper texture, darkened + slightly blurred edges, text on top.
3. Paintings + paper/paint texture + text around the subject + quick camera slide-in.
4. Line + text animations: an accent-blue line draws, text interrupts it, camera tracks the line.
5. Line + illustrations (line leads to objects; zigzag for progressions).
6. Text-centric scenes: textured background, big quotation marks, text in the middle.
7. Centre illustration with text around it.
8. Side illustration + text (two elements at once).
9. Object + text callouts.
10. Graphs/stats: dark textured background, thin glowing grid, a ball tracing the line.

## How he trains Claude on a style
1. Download ~20 of the creator's best videos.
2. Watch each one and ask "why this scene/design/colour/effect/animation?".
3. Screenshot every scene he likes (~130) onto a Figma board, group by principle, annotate fonts/colours/sizes/patterns.
4. Give Claude the 20 videos + the board: transcribe word by word, break down frame by frame (where animations sit in the script, how designs are built, animation timing/feel) → Claude writes a skill + a reference library (225 animations with keyframe sheets).

## How he edits with it
1. Raw clip in → millisecond transcript, silences cut (30 ms padding).
2. Claude makes a PLANNING DOCUMENT: script on one side, recommended scene designs (from the reference library) on the other; the human picks scenes + writes notes (~15 min). Rule he follows: when a new idea is introduced stay on the face; when it's described, show it.
3. Claude builds everything in Remotion, fetching assets itself (Wikipedia paintings, Pexels stock, Google Fonts) → first draft (~60 min), "60–70% there".
4. Specific numbered revisions (~10 min), then sound design + music matched to the references (~15 min). Total ~2 h.

## What we can take into our editor
- Train a style from references: drop 10–20 ads you love in references/<brand>/ → Claude breaks them down into a style file (cuts, captions, sound, animations) before editing.
- Add a planning step: a short plan (script line → shot/graphic/sound) you approve BEFORE the render, instead of fixing after.
- Sound design picked from what the references do.
- For talking-head (personal brand): ms transcript + silence cutting + "face when introducing, visual when describing".
